**So the head-term gap is not a quality problem. It is an age and authority problem.**
That is worth knowing because it changes what you should spend the next six months on.

## Two completely different competitive sets

This is the most useful thing in this document. Your queries split in two, and they need
opposite strategies.

### Set A — "web/app/software development company in Kanpur"
**Opponents:** local Kanpur/Lucknow agencies (SigmaIT, Riveyra, Vyapar, Webguard, CGTech),
plus directories and listicles (WebHopers, topappdevelopmentcompanies.com, Clutch,
GoodFirms).

Old domains, weak sites. Winnable, but on a 12–24 month horizon — you cannot out-age them
this year. Progress here comes from links and citations, not content.

### Set B — "HRMS / payroll / billing / inventory / ERP software in Kanpur"
**Opponents are not local agencies at all.** They are national SaaS products running
programmatic city pages: Marg ERP, factoHR, SoftwareSuggest, InfoGird, Efox, SGCMS.

This is where **you already rank #1 on two queries** — and now I know why. Those companies
sell a *subscription product*. You sell a *custom build you own outright, from someone in
Govind Nagar you can visit*. For a Kanpur factory owner, that is a genuinely different
offer, and their generic city page cannot make it.

**Your `/locations/kanpur/*` pages beat a templated national city page on every axis that
matters locally.** This is the winnable half of the market and it is where the next pages
should go.

Confirmed working: your `/locations/kanpur/erp-software` page now surfaces in search for
ERP + Kanpur + payroll queries.

## What competitors have that you don't

Only three things, and two are free.

**1. Directory and listicle presence.** Every Set A search returns aggregators — WebHopers,
topappdevelopmentcompanies.com, SoftwareSuggest, Clutch, GoodFirms. Your competitors appear
in these. You largely don't. This is both a ranking signal and a second surface where
buyers find you.
→ Clutch profile exists but is unclaimed. GoodFirms doesn't exist. Both free.

**2. Domain age.** Nothing to be done. Time only.

**3. City-page breadth.** SigmaIT covers five cities. You cover one.
→ **Do not copy this.** Their pages are templated and they don't have offices in those
cities either. Building Lucknow/Varanasi pages with no presence is the doorway-page pattern
Google penalises, and it would put your one genuine advantage — being *actually* in
Kanpur — on the same footing as their fake one. Depth in Kanpur beats breadth you can't back.

## What you have that none of them do

Worth stating plainly, because these are defensible and none of your competitors can copy
them quickly:

- **A real Kanpur office**, while the #1 ranker's address is in Lucknow
- **53 real Google reviews at 5.0** — no competitor in this set displays any rating at all
- **Stated prices.** Not one competitor states a price anywhere. For a buyer comparing five
  agencies, you are the only one who answers the first question they have
- **SEO 100 / Accessibility 100 / CLS 0** — best in the set, by a distance
- **Named, verifiable client work** with live links
- **AI-search readiness.** llms.txt, clean structured data, extractable facts. None of them
  have thought about this yet, and it is the one channel where a nine-month-old domain is
  not automatically behind

---

## What to do, in order

**1. Fix the duplicate analytics tags.** Free, and the single biggest speed win available —
334 KiB and ~300–400ms of the main thread, currently wasted loading the same scripts twice.
Details in the deploy notes; it's a change in your Google Tag Manager account, not the code.

**2. Client footer credits** (7 sites, already in hand) and **directory listings** — claim
Clutch, create GoodFirms, LinkedIn Company Page, IndiaMART, JustDial, Sulekha. This is the
authority gap, and it is the whole ballgame for Set A.

**3. Build the next Kanpur pages in Set B, not Set A.** You are fighting national SaaS
templates there, not entrenched local agencies, and you win that comparison on substance.
CRM (built today) was the obvious gap. Next: e-commerce development in Kanpur, then
industry-specific long tail — payroll for tannery piece-rate units, GST billing for kirana
and wholesale traders.

**4. Get into the listicles.** The aggregators ranking on page one for Set A queries accept
submissions. Being *in* the listicle that outranks you is faster than outranking it.

**5. Leave Set A head terms alone for now.** "Software development company in Kanpur"
against a 12-year-old domain is a 2027 target. Trying to force it in 2026 means spending
effort where age decides the outcome.

## Method, so you can check this

- **Speed/SEO/accessibility:** Google PageSpeed Insights API v5, mobile strategy, run
  2026-08-23. Reproducible with the API key now configured.
- **Domain ages:** WHOIS creation dates.
- **Page contents:** fetched and read directly, not inferred from search snippets.
- **Competitor set:** taken from the companies actually returned for "best software
  development company in Kanpur" and "ERP software company Kanpur billing inventory HRMS
  payroll" on 2026-08-23.

**Limitations, stated honestly:** search results are personalised and geo-dependent, and
these were run from this machine, not from Kanpur — a Kanpur searcher may see a different
order, and the local map pack is not visible here at all. No rank-tracking or backlink tool
is configured, so "they outrank you on links" is inference from domain age and directory
presence, not a measured link count. A free Moz key would settle it.
