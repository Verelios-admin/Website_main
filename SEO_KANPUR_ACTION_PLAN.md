## STEP 1 — Reviews (biggest ongoing lever)

You're already collecting Google reviews (42 at 5.0 — great). To turn that into ranking:

- **Keep a steady drip**, not bursts. A few new reviews every week signals an active,
  trusted business. Aim for 2–4 new reviews/week.
- **Reply to every single review** within a day or two — even a one-line thank-you.
  Replies are a ranking signal and show you're active.
- **Nudge keywords naturally.** When asking, suggest clients mention *what* you did and
  *where*: e.g. "We built a website with a software company in Kanpur." Reviews that
  contain your service + city help you rank for those exact searches. Never fake or
  incentivise reviews — Google penalises that.
- **Ask at the happy moment** — right after launch/delivery, over WhatsApp, with the
  direct review link.

**WhatsApp request template:**
> Hi [Name], it was great building [project] with you! If you have 30 seconds, a quick
> Google review would mean a lot — it helps other Kanpur businesses find us. Here's the
> direct link: [your Google review link]. Thank you! 🙏

## STEP 3 — Google Business Profile activity (weekly)

Your profile is well set up; keep it *active* — activity is a ranking factor.

- **Photos:** add a shop-front/exterior photo (Google is asking for it), plus interior,
  team, and screenshots of recent work. Add a few new photos monthly.
- **Google Posts:** publish one short post per week. Rotate topics:
  - A recent project ("New website launched for a Kanpur [industry] business")
  - An offer ("Free 48-hour mockup — book this week")
  - A tip ("3 signs your business needs a custom app")
  - A service spotlight (ERP, payroll, AI automation)
- **Products/Services:** make sure every service is listed with a short description
  (Website development, Mobile apps, Custom software, ERP, HR & payroll, AI automation,
  Web hosting).
- **Q&A:** post 4–5 common questions yourself and answer them (owner Q&A is allowed and
  useful — seed the same ones from your website FAQ).
- **Secondary categories:** add any that are genuinely true (e.g. "Website designer",
  "Software company", "Internet marketing service") — each one lets you appear for more
  searches.

## STEP 4 — Local backlinks (slow but powerful)

A few quality local links raise your whole domain's authority:

- Kanpur / UP business associations, chambers of commerce, startup groups.
- Local news or blog features (offer a comment/story as a local tech founder).
- Partner/vendor sites (clients, tools you resell) linking back to you.
- Guest posts on Indian tech/startup blogs, linking to a relevant page.
- Sponsor a local event/college hackathon → link from their site.

Quality beats quantity — five relevant local links beat fifty spammy ones.
